## 1. ICP (first 50 paying-intent users)

**Primary: solo founders & 2–10-person startup operators** (the person who *is* sales + ops + support).
- Pain: inbound leads rot for hours; mornings eaten by triage; tools (Lindy/Zapier) require building workflows they never build.
- Why them: highest tolerance for AI autonomy, live on X (distribution = acquisition), the inbound-operator flow maps 1:1 to revenue.
- Watering holes: X (build-in-public/AI-tools graph), r/macapps, r/SaaS, Indie Hackers, YC/founder Slacks.

**Secondary: agency owners & fractional execs** (recruiters, marketing agencies, consultants) — every lead has direct $ value; they'll pay first.

**Disqualify for beta:** enterprises, regulated industries, Windows-only users.

## 2. Customer & deal pipeline (using the sales plugin)

Weekly operating loop, runnable in Cowork:

| Step | Skill / tool | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Build target list | `sales:account-research` + Apollo/Clay/ZoomInfo connector (connect one) | 100 named founders/operators matching ICP |
| 2. Research each | `sales:account-research` (web-only works today) | Per-prospect intel: what they post, their stack, trigger events |
| 3. Outreach | `sales:draft-outreach` | Personalized X DM (primary) + email (fallback) per prospect — offer: "50 concierge seats, I onboard you personally, it runs your mornings for a week" |
| 4. Demo calls | `sales:call-prep` before, `sales:call-summary` after | Brief + follow-up + CRM log |
| 5. Track | `sales:pipeline-review` weekly (CSV or Close CRM connector) | Stale-deal flags, weekly action plan |
| 6. Forecast | `sales:forecast` monthly | Commit/upside once there's revenue |

**Cadence:** 20 DMs/week, hand-personalized from research → expect ~15–25% reply at this personalization level → 3–5 demos/week → 50-seat beta filled in ~6–8 weeks. Price the beta ($29–49/mo, founder's-rate-for-life) — free users don't give real feedback on autonomy trust.

**To connect when ready:** one enrichment source (Apollo or Clay), one CRM (Close), Gmail + Calendar (engineering plugin connectors) for scheduling.

## 3. X content engine

**Voice:** confident operator, dry wit, receipts-first. Show, never explain. (Adapt the IDLE villain voice down 20% — Cluely showed pure provocation implodes; provocation + working demo compounds.)

**Setup (one-time):**
1. Run `brand-voice:generate-guidelines` over `growth/` docs + this plan → Agentlyne voice guide.
2. Every post thereafter drafted via `marketing:draft-content` + checked with `brand-voice:enforce-voice` (or `marketing:brand-review`).
3. Schedule a daily Cowork task: "draft 3 posts from yesterday's receipts → my approval queue." Posting stays manual (your account, your send button) until the autonomous-poster system from `growth/IDLE-autonomous-poster.md` is pointed at Agentlyne.

**Content pillars (weekly mix):**
- **Receipts (4×/wk):** 30-sec screen clips — trigger fires → agent plans → multi-step run → finished artifact. Caption = what you *didn't* do. ("I was at the gym. It qualified the lead, booked the call, updated the CRM. Receipt below.")
- **Category war (2×/wk):** assistants vs. operators. "Your AI waits for instructions. That's the bug."
- **Build-in-public (2×/wk):** real numbers — interrupt precision, completions/week, beta count.
- **The Experiment (when live):** agent posts its own progress toward its public signup goal.

**First 10 posts (drafts — run through brand review before posting; all numbers are placeholders until real metrics exist — never post a number you can't screenshot):**
1. "Every AI assistant has the same bug: it waits for you. We removed the waiting."
2. [clip] "9:04am. I hadn't opened my laptop. It had already triaged 31 things and queued 2 decisions. That's the whole product."
3. "Copilots sit next to you while YOU work. Cute. Wrong direction."
4. "A lead filled the form at 2:13am. By 2:14: enriched, qualified, reply drafted, call slot held. I approved it with my coffee."
5. "We don't have workflows. It plans its own steps. Every run is different because every situation is."
6. "Automation tools: you build the flow, it replays it. Agentlyne: it figures out the flow. That's the entire difference."
7. [receipt card] "What it saw → what it did → what I got. Every run ships a receipt. Trust isn't a vibe, it's a log."
8. "50 seats. I onboard you personally. It runs your mornings for a week. If it doesn't finish real work, don't pay."
9. "The cursor-buddy era is AI watching you work. The operator era is you watching AI work. Pick your era."
10. "It asked for permission once today. Everything else it just… did. The approval rate is the metric nobody else will publish. Ours: 74%."
