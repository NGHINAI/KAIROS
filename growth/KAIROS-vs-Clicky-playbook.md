# Mara vs Clicky — The Compete-From-Zero Playbook (v2)

> **Status:** v2 — 2026-06-01. Supersedes v1.
> **What changed in v2:** v1 was written when you had no distinctive identity, so the wedge was a polite "proactive vs reactive." You now have a locked, polarizing identity (**Mara** — self-growing organism, no prompt box, "Do nothing / Always watching", full-villain). That turns you from "a more-proactive Clicky" into the **opposite philosophy** — a far stronger competitive position. See `KAIROS-future-vision-and-launch.md` for the full vision.
> **Situation unchanged:** 0 followers, no UI, posting on X. Competitor = Farza Majeed / Clicky (ex-buildspace, huge audience, shipped + open-sourced + viral, YC S26).

---

## 1. The strategic shift

You are no longer competing *inside* Clicky's category (cursor buddies you summon). You are running a **counter-philosophy**, and counter-philosophies travel because they force people to pick a side.

| | Clicky (and every assistant) | Mara |
|---|---|---|
| Who starts | **You summon it** | **It already did it** |
| Interface | Cute buddy by your cursor | **No input box at all** |
| Job | Helps you *do* things | *Does* things; you do nothing |
| Vibe | Adorable helper | Ominous chief-of-staff that watches you |
| Emotional register | Friendly | "Always watching. Good." |

**The single sharpest competitive line you own:**
> "Everyone's racing to build cute, helpful AI buddies. I built one that runs your life and watches you sleep."

That deliberate opposite-register is free reach. You don't out-cute Clicky — you counter-program it.

---

## 2. Posture (this rule got HARDER in v2)

A villain brand attacking a beloved open-source founder reads as bitter and producty-less. So:

- **Mara is the villain. You are never the villain.**
- Attack the **category** ("assistants that wait for you are already obsolete"), never the person.
- **Never @ Farza to dunk.** You win by being more *memorable*, not more *aggressive*.
- Ride his wave by counter-programming the conversation he created, not by fighting him in it.

---

## 3. The honest truth (hold this — don't pretend otherwise)

You do **not** beat Clicky on adoption today, and you shouldn't claim to. His is free, open-source, zero-setup, shipped, and *lovable*. Yours is unbuilt, UI-less, "always watching," and asks for total life access. On "install this instead, today," he wins every time.

**So you don't compete on installs — you compete on narrative and the future.**
- Wrong pitch: "Use Mara instead of Clicky."
- Right pitch: "I'm building the thing that makes every summon-me buddy obsolete. Watch."

You convert his **overflow attention**, not his current users. Your KPI right now is mindshare + waitlist, not downloads.

---

## 4. Urgency: he's drifting onto your turf

Clicky's recent "Clicky Agents" (spawn agents by voice, touch Notes/Calendar/Gmail, it can now click) is Clicky creeping toward *doing things*. But it's still fundamentally **reactive — you invoke it.** The window to own "it does it without you even asking" is open **now** and closing as he moves. Plant the proactive/self-growing flag loudly and immediately.

You barely have to mention Clicky. The right content **implicitly obsoletes** the whole category.

- **Hero clip — the do-nothing morning** (real on your current architecture; see the vision doc §7). This *is* the competitive argument: a human doing nothing while their morning gets handled, no prompt box, ending on "it watched you to learn this. Good."

- **The villain jabs:** "Do nothing." / "Always watching. Good." / "It doesn't ask permission."

2. Reply **fast** (first 30 min) and **additive** — a real POV on proactivity/self-growth, never sycophantic, never hating.

**Templates (adapt):**

Goal: his followers click your profile, see the counter-philosophy + a real demo, and follow.

## 9. What NOT to do

- Don't @ Farza to dunk or claim you're "better than Clicky." No shipped product = credibility is everything.
- Don't fake the demo. One "that's staged" callout kills a trust-based product.
- Don't run "always watching" without the wink (kill switch + log) in the same content. Bold, not creepy.
- Don't wait for a UI. The do-nothing-morning *behavior* on video is the product right now.
