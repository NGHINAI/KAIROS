## 5. The honesty spine (non-negotiable)

The Experiment is a glass house — "everything is public" + an autonomous poster + a live scoreboard. That's your **strength** (nobody can accuse you of faking) until you fake one thing, at which point the glass shatters.

- **Real commits, real waitlist numbers, real onboardings only.** No invented users or metrics, ever.
- **Villain personality = free. Fabricated facts = forbidden.** "I organized a human's chaos today" (vibe) ✅ / "onboarded 2 users today" when untrue ❌.
- **Replies gated at launch** (adversarial bait kills bots). IDLE posts freely; replies open once proven.
- The trust mechanics (kill switch, "show me what it did" log, undo) are on-screen — the wink that lets you go full-villain without losing trust.
