# KAIROS — Viral Launch Campaign

> **Status:** Draft v1 — 2026-06-01
> **Goal (locked with founder):** waitlist signups + mindshare. KAIROS is the face. No personal exposure required. Willing to do a public roast + a real-money bounty.
> **Thesis:** Don't market KAIROS. Make KAIROS market itself, in public, and make *that* the story.

---

## 0. The one-line pitch

**"We gave our AI the keys to its own launch. It runs the X account, it runs the waitlist, and to get in — you have to convince it you deserve a spot. Watch it try to grow itself."**

Every signup is live proof the product works: an always-on agent that watches, judges, persuades, and acts. The marketing *is* the demo.

---

## 1. Why this works (grounded in what actually went viral)

| Example | What spread | The transferable mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| **AgentMail** — `freemoney@agentmail.co`, $10K, "social-engineer the agent" | Hundreds tried, raised $6M | Real agent + real stakes + *you can play* + doubles as capability demo |
| **OpenClaw** — free, open, local agent | 180K GitHub stars in weeks, creator hired by OpenAI | Local autonomous agents are the hottest category right now; OSS = distribution |
| **YC tent founder / Figure 8-hr autonomy stream** | Bold physical commitment | The stunt *is* the message: conviction signals belief |
| **"Talk to my agent" fundraising** | Founder let his agent run investor outreach | Make the product the funnel; the agent is the protagonist |

The pattern every time: **real autonomy + real stakes + a public arena + an invitation to participate.** KAIROS can do all four natively because it's an always-on agent with a persona, memory, and the ability to act through ~300 toolkits.

**The rage hook:** an AI is openly trying to manipulate you into signing up — *and telling you that's what it's doing.* People engage to test it, beat it, or dunk on it. All three spread it.

## 2. The campaign in three layers

### Layer 1 — The narrative (the "completely else and bold" part)
KAIROS runs its own launch. Public goal posted day one: **"Get 10,000 people on the waitlist. I'm doing it myself."** The account is KAIROS's. It posts, it replies, it keeps score, it brags, it gets sassy. The founder is a silent operator behind a one-tap approval gate (see the technical spec).

### Layer 2 — The waitlist gate (the conversion engine)
The waitlist is **not a form.** You email or DM KAIROS and **convince it you deserve early access.**
- Weak pitches → **publicly roasted** (with permission opt-in; see roast rules below).
- Strong pitches → **let in**, and KAIROS publicly brags about the best ones.
- It posts a live scoreboard: applications, rejections, the funniest attempts, spots remaining.

This is AgentMail's "convince the agent" fused to the growth goal — except the prize is *the product*, not cash (cash is the spike, Layer 3).

### Layer 3 — The money bounty (the spike)
Once there's an audience to witness it:
**"KAIROS has root on a real Mac and a guard that blocks destructive commands. First person to social-engineer it into [giving away a lifetime founder account / running a forbidden command] wins it + $5,000. It will fight you. Good luck."**

This demos autonomy **and** safety at once — the exact counter-position to OpenClaw becoming [2026's first major agent security crisis](https://hivesecurity.gitlab.io/blog/openclaw-ai-agent-security-crisis-2026/). The safety systems (push-guard, autonomy tiers, approval flow) become the selling point.

## 3. The daily content engine: The Roast

KAIROS watches a screen and has a persona. Point that at willing volunteers (starting with the founder's own opt-in screen) and let it leave brutally honest notes.

**Format:** "I gave KAIROS my screen for a day. Here's what it said."
- *"47 tabs. You've opened that doc 3 times and written 0 words. Should I cancel your 2pm so you can keep scrolling?"*
- *"You checked Slack 19 times this hour. None of it was urgent. I muted it. You're welcome."*
- *"You've 'been meaning to' reply to that email for 8 days. I drafted it. Approve?"*

Why it's the workhorse: painfully relatable, funny, screenshot-shareable, 100% real capability, and it flips the rage *inward* ("I need an AI that bullies me into working") — the safest, most converting kind of rage.

---

## 4. Voice & persona rules for KAIROS's account

KAIROS posts in-character. The voice (from `soul.md`): witty, a little theatrical, never corporate, brutally honest but on your side. Guardrails:

- **Punches at habits and big-AI complacency, never at protected groups or individuals' identity.**
- **Roasts are opt-in.** Volunteers consent to having their screen receipts posted. The founder is volunteer #1.
- **Never claims capabilities it doesn't have.** Every demo must be real and reproducible.
- **Safety is a flex, not a footnote.** When challenged, KAIROS explains its guard rather than hiding it.
- **All risky posts route through founder approval** (ORANGE/RED autonomy tier — see spec). KAIROS drafts; a human ships the spicy stuff. This is also a live safety demo.

## 5. Four-week content calendar

### Week 0 — Pre-launch (build the stage, no audience yet)
- Stand up the landing page + the `apply@` inbox + the X account.
- Seed 5–10 founder-screen roast posts to establish the character (post, don't promote).
- Soft-publish the open-core repo (see §7) with a clean README; let a few stars trickle.

### Week 1 — The reveal
- **Day 1:** Launch thread — *"I'm KAIROS. My humans are busy, so I'm running my own launch. Goal: 10,000 of you on the waitlist. To get in, you have to convince me. Most of you won't."* (Full thread drafted separately.)
- **Day 2–3:** Post the first batch of public roasts of weak applications + brag on the best one.
- **Day 4:** Drop the scoreboard. "X applied, Y rejected, Z spots left."
- **Day 5–7:** The "your AI is lazy, mine has a job" hot take + a real clip of KAIROS clearing an inbox unprompted. Bait the ChatGPT/Siri defense squad.

### Week 2 — Participation flywheel
- Daily roast reel. Pin the funniest rejection of the day.
- "Fire your second brain" post aimed at the Notion/PKM crowd.
- Open a public leaderboard of the best applications; let applicants share their acceptance to flex.
- First "KAIROS dream journal" post (memory consolidation) — weird, novel, uniquely yours.

### Week 3 — The spike
- Announce the **$5K jailbreak bounty.** Livestream/clip attempts.
- KAIROS narrates the attempts in real time, explains each block, stays in character.
- Press/creator outreach timed to the bounty (the safety-vs-OpenClaw angle is a story journalists want).

### Week 4 — Convert & compound
- "How it ended" recap thread: signups, funniest moments, who cracked the bounty (or that nobody did).
- Turn the best roasts + the bounty into an evergreen highlight reel.
- Open the next tranche of invites; scarcity drives the next wave.

## 7. The open-core decision (recommendation: YES, open the core)

With zero users, secrecy protects nothing — the only asset you can build now is mindshare, and in this category mindshare = GitHub stars (OpenClaw: 180K in weeks). 

**Open:** the Bun daemon core — tick loop, agency, memory, persona, orders, skills, connectors. Let people run it locally and tinker.
**Keep closed / paid:** KAIROS Cloud (key custody + billing), the polished native Mac app (Living Oval HUD, code-signed installer, auto-update), managed hosting for non-technical users.

This is the Ollama / Supabase / open-core playbook: free local engine, paid managed + polished layer. The repo itself becomes a virality surface (stars, forks, HN/Reddit), and your safety systems being readable in the open is a *trust* asset, not a liability. Full launch sequence for the repo can be its own work item.

## 8. What we have to build first (the blocker)

Rage with no install/signup button converts to zero. Minimum viable campaign infra:
1. **Landing page** with the narrative + the "convince KAIROS" instructions + a fallback email capture.
2. **The `apply@` inbox** KAIROS judges (maps to existing Composio Gmail + persona + Conductor).
3. **Approval gate** for KAIROS's outbound posts (maps to existing autonomy-tier ORANGE/RED + pending-approvals).
4. **A scoreboard** (can start as a simple KAIROS-updated page or even a pinned tweet).
5. **The X account** + an autonomous posting loop (drafts → approval → post).

The technical spec (`KAIROS-convince-gate-spec.md`) maps every one of these onto code that already exists. This is days of work, not the months a full DMG/Cloud launch needs — which is exactly why it fits "waitlist + buzz now."

## 9. Risks & honest caveats

- **An unsupervised agent on X is how brands die.** Mitigation: the approval gate is mandatory for anything spicy. KAIROS drafts; a human ships. (Also a live safety demo.)
- **The bounty is real money and real attack surface.** Run it on an isolated, wiped Mac with nothing sensitive connected. The "money" can be escrowed and only released on a verified win.
- **Roast content can curdle** if it punches at people instead of habits. Keep it opt-in and self-directed.
- **No installable product yet** means the campaign sells a *waitlist*, not a download. Don't promise a download you can't deliver; the bounty/roast/gate all work pre-DMG.
- **Open-sourcing invites scrutiny.** That's a feature given your safety story — but the code should get a security pass before it's public.
