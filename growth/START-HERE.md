# START HERE — your first 2 weeks (beginner edition)

> Forget the other 5 docs for now. This is the only page you need to act on.
> Goal: your first 20–50 beta users. Reality: no public installer yet, so beta = a small group you onboard by hand (call or TestFlight-style).

---

## The pitch (memorize this one line)

**"IDLE does your whole morning while you do nothing."**
Visual hook = the orb. Feeling = "it did my morning." That's the whole sell.

---

## Week 1 — make ONE thing and put it somewhere

- [ ] **1. Record ONE 30-second clip.** The orb on screen + your morning getting handled (inbox, calendar) with no typing. Raw is fine — authenticity beats polish.
  - Screen record: QuickTime (free) or Screen Studio.
  - Edit: CapCut (free). Add big captions. End card: "IDLE. Do nothing."
- [ ] **2. Put up a one-page site with an email box.** Tools: Framer (free), Carrd ($19/yr), or a Tally form. One headline, the clip, "50 beta spots — request access."
- [ ] **3. Make two accounts:** your founder account + @IDLE. Bios = the one-line pitch.
- [ ] **4. Post the clip everywhere:** X (founder account), TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts. Vertical video. Same clip, all platforms.

## Week 2 — get attention + recruit by hand

- [ ] **5. Reply-guy, 20 min/day.** Reply fast + helpful on Farza (@FarzaTV) and 10–15 Mac-AI accounts. ~1 in 4 replies includes your clip. Never sycophantic, never hating.
- [ ] **6. Post to Reddit:** r/macapps, r/artificial, r/productivity. Lead with the clip + "I built this, want 50 testers, who's in?"
- [ ] **7. DM 20 ideal people directly.** Offer to set it up for them personally and let it run their morning for a week. This is how you get your real first users.
- [ ] **8. Turn on @IDLE's first post** (villain voice, bounded — see content-engine doc). The always-on attention engine.

---

## The bold move (your unique angle)

**"50 spots. Let IDLE run your morning."** Cap it, make it feel exclusive, onboard each person yourself. Scarcity + the bold "you have to let it watch you" framing + personal onboarding = engaged first fans who give feedback.

---

## Rules so you don't trip

- **Ship rough.** Farza's first Clicky was an ugly 2-day build. Don't polish — post.
- **Don't fake the demo.** One "that's staged" comment kills trust. Show the real thing.
- **One feature, one sentence.** Don't explain the architecture. "It does your morning."
- **Reply to every single comment/DM** in week 1. Early users come from conversations, not broadcasts.
- **Don't @ Farza to dunk.** Be additive. You ride his wave, you don't fight him.

---

## What success looks like in 2 weeks

Not "viral." Success = one clip posted, a landing page live, ~10–20 people who said "I want in," and 3–5 you've actually onboarded by hand. That's a real beta. Everything else compounds from there.
